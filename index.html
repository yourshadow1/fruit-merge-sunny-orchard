About this policy

This policy explains how Fruit Merge: Sunny Orchard, published under the Fruit Merge Studio label, handles information. It applies to the Android game with package name com.fruitmerge.prototype.

The game works offline, does not require an account, and contains no advertisements or in-app purchases. We do not collect or share personal information through the game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score, discovered fruits, merge counts, current game, remaining skill uses, tutorial status, and music, sound, haptics and motion preferences in Android application-private storage. Local recovery copies help protect progress if a save is interrupted.

These files stay on your device. They are not uploaded to us, synchronized to a cloud account, sold, or shared with advertisers. The game disables its Android cloud-backup and device-transfer backup rules.

Permissions and third-party components

The vibration permission is used for optional haptic feedback. The game does not request Internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or advertising-ID permissions.

The game uses local components for its interface, storage, audio and physics, with bundled fonts and artwork. It does not integrate advertising, analytics, social-login, or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not automatically download fonts. Open-source license notices are available in Settings > Licenses.

Optional support email and external links

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and the content you send so we can answer your inquiry. Sending an email is optional and takes place in your chosen email app; the game does not automatically attach logs, device identifiers, or game files. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Support messages are used to respond to requests and are kept only as long as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of a support message, subject to those obligations.

Choosing to read this policy online opens your browser. This static policy website has no advertising, analytics scripts, or application tracking cookies. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process normal website request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's own privacy statement. Your browser, email provider, device operating system and Google Play operate under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you reset it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the game. Settings > Reset progress clears game progress, scores, discoveries, the saved game and game-recovery copies, while keeping your sound, haptics and motion preferences.

To remove all local information, including preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > Fruit Merge > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the game. Menu names may vary by device. Clearing storage or uninstalling permanently removes local progress. There is no online game account or server-side game profile to delete.

Security

Local saves use Android's application-private storage protections. We do not operate a game-data server. Protecting access to your device helps protect your local progress. No system can provide an absolute security guarantee.

Intended audience

The game is intended for general casual-puzzle players aged 13 and older. It is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not request age, identity, or other personal information inside the game.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a support email, contact us so we can address the request and delete the information where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy and the relevant store declarations if the game's information practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
